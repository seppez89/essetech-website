About Essetech

Essetech is an Adelaide business that looks after IT support, managed IT, cybersecurity, cloud, secure AI and websites for small and medium businesses. It’s run hands-on by its founder, Jonathon Battifuoco.

How we work

  • Our prices are published, in Australian dollars, and exclude GST. You’ll find them on our pricing page.
  • There are no lock-in contracts for IT support or managed IT.
  • We reply to new enquiries within one business day.
  • For support requests, we aim to respond within 30 minutes and put anything that stops your team working first.
  • On-site support is available across the Adelaide metro area, from our base in Brooklyn Park. Remote support is available across Australia.
  • Essetech is a Microsoft AI Cloud Partner and a Microsoft CSP reseller, so we can supply your Microsoft 365 licences and manage them along with your other IT.
