What small businesses automate first

We build around the jobs your team actually does. These are the ones we start with most often.

Answering the phone and replying to enquiries

AI can answer calls that would ring out, take the details and book the job. It can send a text or email reply within 60 seconds of a web enquiry and ask the right questions. Our AI Growth System covers this, and everything we build for it is managed for you.

Following up old leads

If you have a list of old enquiries nobody has followed up, that list is already paid for. We set up personal messages to that list and book replies into your diary.

Answering the same customer questions

An assistant on your website can answer from your own pricing, policies and services. It hands over to a person when it matters.

Quotes, reports and tender responses

If your team writes documents all day, we automate the repeatable parts. Examples include a monthly client report, a quote pack and a tender response. Your people review the output.

Pulling it together for the person at the top

We built an example AI command centre that reads email, Xero, Teams and the calendar overnight and hands a managing partner one ranked briefing in the morning. The walkthrough on our blog uses a fictional firm and invented data. The integrations are real. Read AI for accounting firms.

Security and data handling

We're an IT and cybersecurity company based in Adelaide, so we look after the security side as well as the automation. AI that touches client or customer data has to be set up carefully. We deploy enterprise-grade tools inside your own secured environment, with access controls and an AI use policy your staff sign. Our approach is aligned to Australian privacy law, and your data isn't used to train public models.

We're vendor-agnostic. Most projects use Microsoft 365 Copilot, Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini, chosen to fit the systems you already pay for. For the full approach, see secure Copilot and AI rollout across Australia.
