When your coordinator is stuck in the middle

Your facilities or operations coordinator already has properties, fleet and suppliers to run. IT keeps landing on them anyway, because someone has to talk to the IT provider. They raise the tickets, approve the quotes and explain the problems, without the background to know whether the answer they get back is any good.

Spread that across several sites and it adds up fast. Meanwhile the board still wants to know the IT is in hand.

It's a good fit if:

  • You pay an external IT provider and aren't sure what you're getting for the money.
  • A coordinator or manager has ended up as the go-between with your IT provider.
  • You run several sites and nobody has one list of the devices, logins and internet services across them.
  • Your board has asked about cyber security and you couldn't answer with confidence.

What I look after as your IT manager

I work for you, sitting between your organisation and the IT provider, so your coordinator can get back to facilities.

Your IT provider

I manage them for you. I check their work against what you pay for, raise issues, review contracts and renewals, and make sure projects get delivered. You keep the provider for helpdesk and day-to-day support.

Microsoft 365

I check multi-factor authentication, review who has admin rights and keep email and shared files in order. Essetech is a Microsoft CSP reseller, so I can review your licensing and supply licences directly if that saves you money.

Devices across sites

One list of what's at each site: laptops, PCs, phones, printers and internet services. New devices get set up properly, and replacements get planned and budgeted.

Logins and access

Staff and volunteers come and go. I set people up when they start, remove access that's no longer needed, and write down the admin accounts and who owns them.

Backups and security

Endpoint protection on your machines, email filtering and patching out of hours. I test backups by restoring from them, so you know they work.

Board reporting and planning

A written plain-English report on security, risks, projects and spend, plus a 12 to 24 month IT plan with a budget behind it. I can present it at board meetings and answer questions in the room.

For the full detail of what a month includes, see my fractional IT manager service.

AI help with the admin load

Not-for-profits do a lot of writing and reporting with small teams. I set AI up inside your own secured environment, and your staff check what it drafts. The information you hold about the people you support is often sensitive, so I start with admin that doesn't touch it, and staff get a written AI use policy that says what can and can't go into it.

Grant applications

Drafting applications from your own program information and past submissions, ready for someone to check and tidy.

Reports for the board and funders

Turning the monthly numbers and program notes into a plain-English draft report.

Shared inboxes

Filing emails to shared inboxes like admin or finance by topic, with a short summary of what's new.

Member and supporter updates

Drafting newsletters and updates in your organisation's voice.

I start with one job that's clear and worth the effort, and build it. You see it working before you commit to more. If it earns its place, I build the next one.
