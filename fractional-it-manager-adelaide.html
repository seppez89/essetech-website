Who a fractional IT manager suits

This service is built for Adelaide businesses with roughly 10 to 80 staff and no IT manager. Usually someone in the business has ended up owning IT on top of their real job: the office manager, the finance lead or one of the owners.

They approve invoices from the IT provider without knowing whether the price is right, and nobody has a plan for the next two years.

It's a good fit if:

  • You pay an IT provider or several vendors and aren't sure what you're getting for the money.
  • A client, insurer or auditor has asked about your cyber security and you couldn't answer with confidence.
  • You have a project coming up, such as an office move, a Microsoft 365 migration or an AI rollout, and nobody to run it.
  • Your board or owners want regular, plain reporting on IT risk and spend.

What a month includes

Days on site

You get a set number of days at your office each month. Staff can raise problems face to face, and we can see how people actually work. Between visits we're available by phone and email for questions and decisions.

Vendor management

We deal with your IT provider, internet and phone carriers, software vendors and printer leases for you. That means reviewing contracts and renewals, checking that support tickets are handled properly, and pushing back on quotes that don't make sense. We're a Microsoft AI Cloud Partner and a CSP reseller through Leader Systems, so we can also review your Microsoft licensing and buy it directly if that saves you money.

Microsoft 365 and security

We review your Microsoft 365 setup, multi-factor authentication, admin accounts, backups and device management against the Essential Eight. You get a list of gaps ranked by risk, and we fix them or get your provider to fix them. Each month we check that what was fixed stays fixed.

IT strategy and planning

We write a 12 to 24 month IT plan that covers hardware replacement, software, security work, AI adoption and budget. It's tied to what the business is trying to do, so spending decisions have a reason behind them.

Board and management reporting

You get a written report in plain English covering security status, open risks, project progress and IT spend. We can present it at board or management meetings and answer questions in the room.

How a fractional IT manager differs from an MSP

A managed service provider (MSP) runs your helpdesk, monitors devices and fixes things when they break, usually for a monthly fee per user. That work still needs doing, and most of our clients keep their MSP.

An MSP has no reason to tell you its own contract is too expensive, or that a project it quoted for isn't needed. A fractional IT manager works for you. We set the priorities, check the MSP's work against the agreement and make the calls an in-house IT manager would make.
