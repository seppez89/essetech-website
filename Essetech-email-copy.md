### Sending notes
- The HTML file embeds three images as base64 so it previews anywhere: the official rocket mark (`essetech-mark.png`), the background-removed figurine (`figurine-cutout.png`), and your photo (`jonathon-circle.png`). Before a real send, host these on your site and swap the `src` values to hosted URLs so the email loads fast and survives forwarding. The big base64 payload can also trip spam filters, so hosted images are safer at scale.
- Signature uses your real details: Jonathon Battifuoco, Founder & IT Consultant, 0404 311 791, jonathon@essetech.com.au.
- Pair a subject line with a different preview text (do not repeat the same idea twice).
- For cold sends, include a physical address and one-click unsubscribe to stay compliant with the Spam Act.
