When the office manager is also the IT person

In a trade business with 5 to 30 staff there's rarely anyone whose job is IT, so it lands on whoever sits in the office. They set up the new apprentice's phone, reset passwords, chase the internet provider and work out why the printer won't scan to email.

None of that is what you hired them for. And when they're on leave, nobody else knows where the passwords are.

It's a good fit if:

  • Your office manager or ops coordinator has become the go-to for IT problems on top of their real job.
  • Nobody's sure who has the admin logins for email, the website or Microsoft 365.
  • The crew use a mix of work and personal phones and old laptops, and you don't have a list of what's out there.
  • You've just hired someone to sort out the office systems and they need someone to call when it's an IT problem.

What I look after as your IT manager

Your job software and Xero stay as they are. I look after everything they run on, so the office can get back to running jobs.

Devices

Office PCs, laptops and the phones and tablets the crew take on site. I keep a list of what you've got, set new ones up properly and plan replacements before something dies on a Monday morning.

Logins and access

When an apprentice starts I set them up. When someone leaves I remove the access they no longer need. Admin accounts get written down along with who owns them, so the passwords don't live in one person's head.

Microsoft 365 and email

I check multi-factor authentication, review who has admin rights and keep email and shared files in order. Essetech is a Microsoft CSP reseller, so I can supply and manage your Microsoft 365 licences as well.

Backups

I check what's being backed up and what isn't. Then I test the backups by restoring from them, so you find out they work before you need them.

Security

I run endpoint protection on your machines, filter email and patch out of hours. Email filtering is there to catch the lookalike invoice emails before anyone in the office pays one.

Vendors

Your internet and phone provider, your software vendors, the printer lease and any IT provider you already pay. I deal with them, check renewals and push back on quotes that don't make sense.

AI help for the office admin

A lot of office work in a trade business follows the same pattern every week. AI is good at that kind of work. I set it up on tools that keep your data safe, and your team checks what it drafts before anything goes out.

Quotes

Turning job notes into a draft quote from your own templates, ready for someone in the office to check and send.

Follow-ups

Polite follow-ups on quotes nobody has answered, and personal messages to old enquiries that never got a call back.

Calls and enquiries

Calls that would ring out while everyone's on the tools can be answered by AI, which takes the details and books the job. Web enquiries get a reply within 60 seconds. My AI Growth System covers this.

The office inbox

Sorting the shared admin inbox so supplier invoices, customer questions and job bookings stop sitting in one pile.

I start with one job that's clear and worth the effort, and build it. You see it working before you commit to more. If it earns its place, I build the next one.
