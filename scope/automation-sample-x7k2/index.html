05 · What we'll build and exactly how

The new automation, step by step.

This is the Docket-to-Claim build for the first crew. It is a proposal for work we will do, not a record of work already done. AI reads the photo. Rules price it. A named person approves. The invoice is drafted on hold, and finance releases it.

How the system is put together

1. Photo, same as today

The supervisor photographs the signed paper docket or timesheet. That habit does not change.

Paper docket · the supervisor's phone

2. Intake

The photo is sent to a shared Outlook mailbox or a Teams channel, so it stays in your Microsoft 365 tenant. Power Automate picks it up and files it in the claims folder in SharePoint.

Microsoft 365 · Power Automate · SharePoint

3. AI reads the fields

A document reader pulls names, hours, plant, work order, signature and handwritten notes off the page. Low-confidence fields are marked for a person. The images are not used to train any model.

A commercial AI document reader, in a region we agree in writing

4. Rules price the claim

Each line is priced from your Santos rate schedule, held in a SharePoint list. Standby, variations and missing approvals are flagged. Pricing is plain rules, not AI.

SharePoint list · rules engine

5. A person checks

A named admin opens the review queue. The photo sits beside the claim lines. They approve, fix, or send it back to the supervisor. Nothing is sent to Santos without this step.

Review queue in SharePoint, opened as a check screen

6. Draft invoice and payroll hours

The invoice is drafted on hold in MYOB Advanced, for finance to release. Hours are sent to Employment Hero for payroll to approve, if API access is ready. Billable hours are not always payable hours.

MYOB Advanced · Employment Hero

7. Claim pack, ready to upload

A portal-ready pack is built, with the signed dockets attached. An admin still uploads it to the Santos portal. There is no portal integration.

A file you upload to the client portal

Where the work runs. Intake, files, the rate list and the review queue sit in your Microsoft 365 tenant. Power Automate runs the flow. The AI reader is a commercial service, in a region we agree in writing, before any Santos document is sent. Accounts stay in MYOB Advanced. Payroll stays in Employment Hero.

06 · Claude Projects for the office team

A place for the questions the pipeline cannot answer.

A Claude Project is a shared workspace with its own knowledge files and standing instructions, so every chat in that Project starts with the same approved documents. This is a proposal for how we would set that up. It is not work already done.

How this sits with the pipeline

The Docket-to-Claim pipeline

Does the repetitive bulk work, automatically. Photos in, claims and draft invoices out, with a person still approving.

Photo→ AI reads→ Rules price
Person checks→ Draft invoice

Claude Projects

Help people with judgement work: contract questions, writing, and disputes. A person still reviews and sends anything that leaves the building.

A question→ Project files
Draft or answer→ Person reviews

They do different jobs. The pipeline processes hundreds of dockets a month. Projects help when someone needs to interpret the Santos contract, draft a letter, or answer "how do we do this?". One does not replace the other.

Six Projects we would set up

1. Santos contract and rates

Used by contracts admin, project engineers and the estimator

What goes in
The current Santos rate schedule, the variation and standby rules, and the agreed contract extracts. Instructions: answer only from those files, quote the clause, and say when it is unclear.

Benefit, as an estimate: 10 to 20 minutes saved on a "can we charge for this?" question.A person still makes the call before anything is claimed.

2. Claims and dispute replies

Used by contracts admin

What goes in
The contract clauses on claims, common bounce reasons, and a few approved reply templates. A redacted sample docket can sit alongside, if ICE agrees. Instructions: draft a reply in ICE's tone, tied to the clause and the docket, and flag anything missing.

Benefit, as an estimate: 30 to 60 minutes saved per bounced-claim reply.A named person still checks and sends it.

3. Quotes and variation letters

Used by the estimator and project engineers

What goes in
The rate schedule, ICE's letter templates, and a short style guide. Instructions: draft from the template and the rates, show the working, and do not invent a rate that is not on the schedule.

Benefit, as an estimate: 20 to 40 minutes saved per letter, because you are not starting from a blank page.The estimator still owns the numbers.

4. Safety and site documents

Used by supervisors and project engineers

What goes in
ICE's own SWMS and JSA templates, and any standing site rules you choose to include. Instructions: draft only from those templates, keep ICE's headings, and mark every draft as needing a person's review and sign-off.

Benefit, as an estimate: 30 to 45 minutes on the first draft of a familiar SWMS or toolbox talk note.This does not replace a signed, site-specific safety document.

5. Supervisor and admin help desk

Used by supervisors and contracts admin

What goes in
The SOPs and the check-screen guide for the new docket process, once those exist. Instructions: give short how-to answers from the SOPs, and say when the person should ask the process owner.

Benefit, as an estimate: fewer "how do I do this?" interruptions in the first months after go-live.This Project is built after the pipeline guide exists, so it stays accurate.

6. Monthly reporting

Used by finance, the GM, and the owner on ICE's side

What goes in
A monthly export of the leak-register and invoicing figures, with no personal or payroll data. Instructions: turn the numbers into a short management summary, keep the working visible, and do not add figures that are not in the file.

Benefit, as an estimate: 1 to 2 hours saved each month packing the numbers into a note.Finance still signs off the figures before they go further.

How we would set it up

1. Team seats

ICE takes a Claude Team plan, in ICE's name. Team is needed so Projects can be shared with the people who will use them. You can keep a Project private to invited people, or open it to the organisation. Each person can view and chat, or edit the files, depending on the permission you set. Their individual chats stay private unless they share them.

2. Build each Project

We curate the knowledge files and write the instructions for each of the six Projects, then test them on real examples you provide. The approved documents live in the Project knowledge, so every chat in that Project starts from the same source.

3. Data rules

No personal data, no payroll records, no tax file numbers and no bank details. We check ICE's Santos contract before any Santos document is loaded. On the Team plan, Anthropic does not train its models on your content by default. ICE remains in control of the account.

4. Microsoft 365, if you want it

Claude has a Microsoft 365 connector that can search SharePoint, OneDrive, Outlook and Teams, using only the access each person already has. A Claude owner in ICE turns it on, a Microsoft administrator grants consent, then each person connects their own account. We would still put the approved files into the Project knowledge, so answers stay on the documents you have signed off. We would not turn on write access (sending mail or editing files) unless ICE asks for that later.

5. Owners and training

Each Project gets a named ICE owner, who decides what is added. We run a short training session for the people who will use them: how to ask, how to check a draft, and what must never go in.

6. Quarterly refresh

When the rate schedule changes, a template is updated, or the docket process changes, the owner and essetech refresh the files and instructions. Without that, the Project will slowly go stale.

What this costs to run. Anthropic's current Team Standard list price, checked on 13 November 2026 against claude.com/pricing and Anthropic's Help Center, is US$20 per seat per month if billed annually, or US$25 per seat per month if billed monthly, excluding tax. The plan has a minimum of two seats. Currency and GST for Australian buyers are shown at checkout. Seats are passed through at cost, on ICE's own Team account. Premium seats cost more and are only needed if someone regularly hits the Standard usage limit.

08 · Automatic invoicing

The invoice is drafted automatically, once a person says yes.

Once the claim is approved inside ICE, the invoice is drafted on hold in MYOB Advanced. Line items come off the docket. Each line is priced from the Santos rate schedule, not by AI, and tied to the right work order and purchase order. The signed dockets are attached. Finance releases the invoice. Nobody types it.
