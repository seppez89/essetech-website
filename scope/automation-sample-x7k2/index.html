How I set it up

  1. Build the two sites. Jobs and Subbies, with the lists and folder layout above.
  2. Lock the private folders first. HR, Payroll and Finance on the Office site are limited to Dan and Mia before anything is connected, because the Claude connector can see whatever a person can see.
  3. Create the automation account. Your IT provider adds it to the Jobs and Subbies sites and the accounts@ inbox, and nothing else.
  4. Connect the apps to it. Xero, DocuSign, SafetyCulture and Claude are connected in Power Automate under that account, so no one's personal login is used.
  5. Everything is logged. Every flow run shows in Power Automate's run history and your Microsoft 365 audit log. Turning off the automation account stops every flow, and your files stay where they are.
