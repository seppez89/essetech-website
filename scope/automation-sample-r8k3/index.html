02 · What we'll build

Photo, check, draft invoice, then a reminder.

Takeaway: AI reads the job sheet, Fergus is filled in, a person checks it, a draft invoice goes to Xero, and Pipedrive sends the follow-up. This is a proposal, not work already done.

Photo, same as today

The technician photographs the job sheet and the site, and sends it to a shared mailbox.

The technician's phone · shared mailbox

AI reads the sheet

Names, hours, materials and notes come off the page. Low-confidence items go to a person. Images are not used to train any model.

A commercial document reader

The job is filled in Fergus

The job record is updated. Extra materials and variations are flagged for a person, not silently billed.

Fergus API

The office checks

The photo sits beside the job lines. Approve, fix, or send it back. Nothing is invoiced without this step.

A check screen in your Microsoft 365

Draft invoice and follow-up

A draft invoice is created in Xero, for a person to release. Pipedrive then sends a review request and sets a next-service reminder.

Xero API · Pipedrive Automations

About the tools. Fergus has a public API and a built-in Xero link. For a draft invoice that a person still releases, we will use the Xero API after the office checks the job. We can use Fergus's own Xero link instead, if you prefer invoices to post from Fergus. Pipedrive Automations, on the Growth plan or higher, can send the review request and the reminder from your own inbox. That email does not post a Google review for you. The Fergus API has a published rate limit per company, which is fine for this volume.

03 · How we'd build it

Six weeks to live, beside the current process.

Takeaway: we test on your past job sheets first. The office keeps doing what it does now until the numbers can be explained. All 12 technicians are in this build.

WhenWe buildGate
Weeks 1–2 Shared mailbox intake, and the reader tested on about 150 past job sheets, plus a held-out set of hard photos. 95% of agreed fields. Paid stop/go. Money fields have a separate target.
Weeks 3–4 Fergus job update, flags for extras and variations, the check screen, and a draft invoice in Xero. One week beside the current process. Every difference from the manual invoice is explained. You sign that pack off.
Weeks 5–6, then 30 days Pipedrive review request and next-service reminder, office training, then go live. 30 days of close support. Live for all 12 technicians, in accounts you own.

What we need from you

  • One owner, about 2 hours a week
  • About 150 past job sheets, and API access to Fergus, Xero and Pipedrive
  • Your materials and rate list, and the wording for the review email
  • A Pipedrive Growth plan or higher, with email sync turned on

Risks we are managing

  • Messy handwriting: low-confidence fields go to a person
  • Job photos stay in your Microsoft 365 tenant. The reader is a commercial service, in a region we agree in writing
  • Extras are flagged, not billed, until a person says yes

06 · Terms in brief

The rules this proposal sits on.

Takeaway: a short statement of work will repeat these terms on sign-off.

Assumptions

  • About 360 jobs a month, on the current job sheet
  • API access to Fergus, Xero and Pipedrive, plus a test company in Xero
  • Pipedrive is on Growth or higher, with email sync
  • A person in the office is available to check jobs

Change control and acceptance

  • Changes are requested in writing. We confirm the effect on price and time before the work starts
  • The gates in the plan are the acceptance tests. You sign each one
  • If a gate is not signed within 10 business days of us asking, and no written defect is raised, that gate is taken as accepted

Data, privacy and security

  • Job photos and files live in your Microsoft 365 tenant. Invoices live in Xero. Job records stay in Fergus
  • The AI provider and region are agreed in writing before any customer job sheet is sent
  • Access uses named accounts, least privilege and MFA. Access is removed at the end

Ownership, warranty and liability

  • Deliverables are assigned to you on payment. essetech keeps a licence to its pre-existing tools. Third-party products stay under their own licences
  • Defects in the delivered work, as against this scope, are fixed at no charge during the 30-day close support window
  • Liability is capped at fees paid under this proposal. Consequential loss is excluded, including missed extras. You remain responsible for approving invoices. essetech holds professional indemnity, public liability and cyber insurance
