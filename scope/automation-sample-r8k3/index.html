The short version

Your technicians finish a job, write it on a paper job sheet and send a photo. Someone in the office then types every sheet into Fergus, and the invoice goes out about nine days later. Along the way, extra parts and variations get missed. We'd have AI read the photo and fill in Fergus, so the office checks instead of types. Extras get flagged, invoices go out in about two days, and every customer gets a review request and a service reminder. Nothing is billed until a person says yes.

04 · What we'd build first

Job Sheet to Invoice: the office checks instead of types, and no extra gets missed.

It works with Fergus, Xero and Pipedrive, which you already use. Your technicians keep doing exactly what they do now: take a photo.

  1. Photo, same as today

    The tech photographs the job sheet and the site and sends it to one inbox. No new app to learn.

    Technician

  2. AI reads the sheet

    Names, hours, parts and notes come off the page. Anything it isn't sure of goes to a person.

    AI

  3. Fergus is filled in

    The job is updated in Fergus. Extra parts and variations are flagged, never billed on their own.

    System

  4. The office checks

    The photo sits beside the job lines. Approve, fix, or send it back to the tech. Nothing is invoiced without this.

    Office

  5. Draft invoice in Xero

    A draft invoice is ready the same day for the office to send. About two days from job done to invoice out.

    System

  6. Follow-up goes out

    Pipedrive emails a review request from your own inbox and sets a reminder for the next service.

    System

07 · Delivery and investment

Six weeks, tested on your own job sheets first.

Nothing touches a live job until the reader has passed a test on your past job sheets. The office keeps working the way it does now until the numbers match.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Reader test

    Set up the shared inbox and test the reader on about 150 of your past job sheets, including the messy ones.

    Gate: 95% of fields read correctly, or you can stop here
  2. Weeks 3–4

    Side by side

    Fergus updates, extra flags, the check screen and draft invoices in Xero, run for one week beside your current process.

    Gate: every difference from your own invoices explained and signed off
  3. Weeks 5–6

    Follow-up and training

    Review requests and service reminders in Pipedrive, then training for the office.

    Gate: the office is happy to switch over
  4. Then

    Live for all 12 techs

    Running in accounts you own, with close support for the first 30 days.

    Go-live, then 30 days of close support

What we need from you

  • One owner on your side, about 2 hours a week

  • Your materials and rates list, and the wording for the review email

08 · The fine print

The terms, in plain words.

A short statement of work repeats these on sign-off.

Changes and sign-off

Changes are asked for in writing, and we confirm the effect on price and time before starting. Each gate in the plan is an acceptance test you sign. If a gate isn't signed within 10 business days and no problem is raised in writing, it counts as accepted.

Your data

Job photos and files live in your Microsoft 365, invoices in Xero and job records in Fergus. The AI provider and region are agreed in writing before any customer job sheet is sent. Access uses named accounts with multi-factor sign-in, and is removed when we finish.

Ownership, warranty and liability

Everything we build is yours once paid. essetech keeps a licence to its own existing tools, and other products stay under their own licences. Defects against this scope are fixed free during the 30 days of close support. Liability is capped at the fees paid under this proposal, and consequential loss, including missed extras, is excluded. You approve your own invoices. essetech holds professional indemnity, public liability and cyber insurance.

Technical notes

Fergus has a public API and its own Xero link. We'll create draft invoices through the Xero API after the office checks each job, or use Fergus's Xero link if you'd rather invoices post from Fergus. Pipedrive automations need the Growth plan or higher. The review email asks the customer for a review. It can't post one.
