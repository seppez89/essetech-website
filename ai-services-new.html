Good questions.

Is our information safe?

Yes. Copilot works inside your own Microsoft 365 and follows the permissions you already have. Before switching anything on, I check who can see which files, so nobody sees something they shouldn't.

Can it send emails without us knowing?

No. Copilot drafts, and a person checks and presses Send. The only thing that sends on its own is the reminder texts, and only to the people on the roster you set.

Do we need new software?

No. Everything here runs in Outlook, Teams, Word and Excel, which your team already uses. Copilot is added for the people who need it.

What if my team isn't technical?

That's who this is built for. Everything is set up for you, training is in plain English, and I'm on hand afterwards.
