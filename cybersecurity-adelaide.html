Real scenarios

What an attack on a business your size looks like

It's rarely a hooded genius. It's these three, and each one has a specific defence that stops it.

1

A fake invoice from a supplier you really use

Same logo, same layout, bank details changed by a couple of digits. Email filtering spots the lookalike domain and quarantines it before it ever reaches your accounts inbox.

2

A staff password turns up in someone else's data breach

People reuse passwords, and attackers try them everywhere. With multi-factor authentication switched on properly, a stolen password on its own gets them nowhere.

3

Ransomware locks the shared drive on a Friday night

Because the backups are automated and tested, you restore from Saturday's copy instead of deciding whether to pay someone.
